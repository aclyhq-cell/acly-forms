The 8 sections of your diagnostic
Cash Flow X-Ray
How every dollar flows in and out. Of every $100 that comes in, how much goes to variable costs, fixed costs and what's actually left as profit.
Financial leaks detected
The costs and situations eating your profit without you seeing it — waste, unadjusted delivery commissions, below-potential ticket, products that drain capacity without margin.
Critical problems identified
The 3–4 most urgent problems in your business, ranked by severity with the economic impact of each — in monthly dollars, not opinions.
Real profitability per product
Which ones actually make money, which barely cover costs and which need to be reconsidered. Plus hidden opportunities you're not taking advantage of.
Exact break-even point
How much you need to sell per day, week and month just to break even — and how close or far you are from that limit today.
Scenario: your business with 3 changes
What would happen to your profit if you adjust costs, prices and average ticket. Side-by-side comparison of your current situation vs real potential — in dollars, not percentages.
Action plan in order of impact
5 concrete moves, ordered by what moves the most money first. Each with estimated monthly dollar impact and realistic time to implement.
Personalized strategic interpretation
What your numbers reveal about how you perceive your business — and where the real blind spots the analysis detected beyond the numbers.
